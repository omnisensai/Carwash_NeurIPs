User goals:
- Perform an activity on an object at a service location.
- No other objectives or goals are relevant for the user.

Definitions:
Object = car
User = human
Service location = car wash

Constraints:
- The object is initially with the user at the same location.
- The activity is performed at the service location.
- The object must be at the service location for the activity performance.
- Vehicles are not portable.
- Walking leaves a vehicle behind.
- Walking does not transport a vehicle.
- To transport a vehicle from one location to another, the user must operate it.
