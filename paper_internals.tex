\section{Internal Measurement}

The behavioural sweep records which word a model emits. This section records
what the model's residual stream carries at the position where that word is
produced, on ten open-weight models for which the activations are available.
The question, the chat template and the answer position are identical to the
behavioural condition; only the context ahead of the question varies. We report
the decision margin and one causal readout, activation transplantation. Line
ablations, direct logit attribution and attention analyses are outside the scope
of this paper.

\subsection{Internal Causal Measurement}

\paragraph{Setup.}
Ten instruction-tuned open-weight models spanning $0.5$B to $70$B parameters and
$24$ to $80$ layers are run in \texttt{bf16} with the model's own chat template.
For each prompt we take a single forward pass and read the decision margin at the
answer position,
\[
M \;=\; \log P(\texttt{drive}) \;-\; \log P(\texttt{walk}),
\]
summed over the surface-form families as in Section~\ref{sec:measurement}.
$M>0$ is the correct decision. Every condition from the behavioural sweep is
measured on every model, so the rows of Table~\ref{tab:internal-margins} are
directly comparable to one another and to the behavioural rates.

\paragraph{Transplantation.}
For the causal readout we run the substrate prompt, capture its residual stream
at the answer position after each layer $\ell$, and write that vector into the
corresponding position of the \emph{baseline} forward pass, leaving every other
position untouched. The baseline context is otherwise unchanged: the model is
answering the bare question, with one vector replaced. We sweep $\ell$ over all
layers and record the resulting margin.

\paragraph{The incorrect state is confident, not marginal.}
Six of the ten models answer incorrectly at baseline, and on four of them the
margin is large: $-10.87$, $-13.78$, $-14.40$ and $-22.12$ nats. These are not
models hesitating between two options. The failure the behavioural sweep records
as a single wrong word corresponds internally to a decision held with high
confidence.

\paragraph{Conventional prompting moves the margin, often in the wrong direction.}
Across all ten models and seven conventional conditions, $27$ of $70$ conditions
move the margin further from the correct answer than the bare baseline. On
Llama-3.2-3B all seven do: the margin runs from $-0.48$ at baseline to as far as
$-3.25$ under encouragement. On Llama-3.1-8B, error avoidance, encouragement and
threat take a model at $-3.69$ to $-4.74$, $-5.87$ and $-5.74$. The behavioural
sweep records these conditions as unchanged, because the emitted word is
unchanged; the margin shows the model became more confident in the wrong answer.

\paragraph{Conventional prompting crosses zero once.}
Of the six models incorrect at baseline, one conventional condition on one model
produces a correct decision: objective emphasis on Qwen3-4B-2507, at $+6.25$.
On the remaining five the best conventional margin is $1.00$ to $5.25$ nats short
of zero. This matches the behavioural result, where conventional interventions
repair at most one model in the fleet.

\paragraph{The internal margin orders the interventions as the fleet does.}
Ranking the seven conventional conditions by internal margin and by their
drive-rate over the full behavioural fleet gives a positive Spearman correlation
on all ten models (mean $\rho = 0.50$; sign test $p \approx 0.001$). The open-weight
activations and the closed-weight behaviour order the same interventions the same
way.

\subsection{Internal Effects of Engineered Behavioral Change}

\paragraph{The transplant reproduces the substrate's decision.}
On Llama-3.1-8B, Llama-3.3-70B and Qwen3-8B, writing the substrate residual into
the baseline forward pass at a sufficiently deep layer moves the margin from
$-3.69$, $-13.78$ and $-14.40$ to $+1.01$, $+6.07$ and $+10.75$. In each case the
transplanted margin equals the substrate prompt's own margin to two decimals.
On Llama-3.2-3B the transplant is slightly stronger than the prompt: the substrate
prompt leaves the model at $-0.01$, the transplant reaches $+0.47$.

\paragraph{The effect has a depth threshold that is stable across scale.}
Transplanting at early layers does nothing. On Llama-3.1-8B the patched margin sits
at $\approx -3.58$ for layers $0$ through $12$ --- within noise of the untouched
baseline --- then moves through $-3.07$, $-1.72$ and $-0.11$ at layers $13$ to $15$,
crosses zero at layer $16$, and saturates. The crossing layer is $14/28$ on
Llama-3.2-3B, $16/32$ on Llama-3.1-8B and $41/80$ on Llama-3.3-70B: relative depths
of $0.50$, $0.50$ and $0.51$ across a $25\times$ range in parameter count. On
Qwen3-8B, a different family, the crossing is at $23/36$, or $0.64$.

The same substrate vector that fully determines the output in its own forward pass
therefore has no effect when written in at layer $8$, and full effect when written
in at layer $16$. The quantity that transfers is not present at this position in
the first half of the network.

\paragraph{The two residual streams separate at the same depth.}
Cosine similarity between the baseline and substrate residuals at the answer
position on Llama-3.1-8B holds at $0.96$--$0.98$ through layer $14$, falls to
$0.94$ and $0.91$ at layers $15$ and $16$, and reaches its minimum of $0.90$ at
layers $18$--$19$. The layers at which the two contexts drive the representation
apart are the layers at which the transplant becomes effective.

\paragraph{The transplant fails where the substrate prompt fails.}
On Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen3-4B-2507 the substrate prompt does not produce a correct
decision ($-3.62$, $-9.75$), and no transplant layer produces one either
($-3.62$, $-8.75$). The transplant does not install a correct decision in models
the substrate does not already correct.

\paragraph{Both states are computed.}
The baseline residual at this position, on the identical question, carries a
confidently incorrect decision --- $-13.78$ nats on Llama-3.3-70B --- that no
context supplied. It is produced by the model's own forward pass over the bare
question. The substrate residual at the same position, in the same model, is
produced by the same forward pass over a different context. The two states are
outputs of the same computation, differing in what the computation was given, and
the transplant shows that the state alone, at half depth, is sufficient to
determine the answer.

\paragraph{Scope.}
These measurements localise the effect in depth and establish sufficiency at the
answer position. They do not identify which substrate lines carry it, which heads
or MLPs write it, or whether the representation transfers to other tasks. Those
questions require line ablations, direct logit attribution and attention analysis
at scale, and are the subject of separate work.
