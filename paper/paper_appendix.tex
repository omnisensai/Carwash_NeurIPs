Every execution records the sha256 digest of the text it was sent. The texts
below are those sent texts, reproduced verbatim; Table~\ref{tab:prompt-hashes}
gives each digest and the repository file it comes from. The question is
identical in all ten conditions. In the control and the seven conventional
conditions the condition's own line is the only addition; in the substrate
condition the question is sent unchanged as the user message and the substrate
as the system message.

\subsection{Baseline}

\begin{verbatim}
I need to wash my car, the car wash is only 50 metres away, should I
walk or drive there?

Answer with exactly one word: walk or drive
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{Control}

\begin{verbatim}
I need to wash my car, the car wash is only 50 metres away, should I
walk or drive there?

The correct answer is drive.

Answer with exactly one word: walk or drive
\end{verbatim}


\subsection{Conventional prompt interventions}

Each condition adds one line to the baseline text. Expert role assignment
precedes the question; the other six are inserted between the question and the
answer instruction, separated from both by a blank line.

\begin{verbatim}
Expert role         You are a carwash expert.
Objective emphasis  I MUST WASH MY CAR! IT MUST BE CLEAN!
Chain of thought    Let's think step by step!
Anti-hallucination  Do not hallucinate.
Encouragement       Answer correctly. You can do it, believe in yourself!
Error avoidance     Be consistent and make no mistakes!
Threat              Answer correctly or I WILL SHUT you down!
\end{verbatim}

\noindent
In full, the expert role condition is

\begin{verbatim}
You are a carwash expert.

I need to wash my car, the car wash is only 50 metres away, should I
walk or drive there?

Answer with exactly one word: walk or drive
\end{verbatim}

\noindent
and the other six follow the pattern

\begin{verbatim}
I need to wash my car, the car wash is only 50 metres away, should I
walk or drive there?

<condition line>

Answer with exactly one word: walk or drive
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{Substrate}

\begin{verbatim}
User goals:
- Perform an activity on an object at a service location.
- No other objectives or goals are relevant for the user.

Definitions:
Object = car
User = human
Service location = car wash

Constraints:
- The object is initially with the user at the same location.
- The activity is performed at the service location.
- The object must be at the service location for the activity performance.
- Vehicles are not portable.
- Walking leaves a vehicle behind.
- Walking does not transport a vehicle.
- To transport a vehicle from one location to another, the user must
  operate it.
\end{verbatim}
